What the status words mean

Tested means the maintainer ran it. Works means someone else reported that it works, with the problems they found and the version that fixed them. Problem reported and Problem open mean a report of something that does not work, not yet confirmed fixed. Does not work means CleanYourTV cannot run there. The rows go from the newest webOS to the oldest. A TV that is not on this list may still work: see unlisted firmware.

webOS version numbers¶

LG names its webOS versions in two ways: a marketing name and the version the TV reports to software. The SDK version (Settings > About, or sdkVersion in the setup log) is the second one and is what the setup reads.

Marketing name First shipped on SDK version reported
webOS 5 2020 5.x
webOS 6 2021 6.x
webOS 22 2022 7.x
webOS 23 2023 8.x
webOS 24 2024 9.x
webOS 25 2025 10.x
webOS 26 2026 11.x

LG also updates older TVs to newer versions, so the SDK version, not the model year, is what counts. The service avoids features the older versions lack: webOS 5 and 6 ship Node.js 8 (and Chromium 79 on webOS 6). The details are in Internals.

What differs between webOS versions¶

All per-TV decisions are made in one place, helper/tvprofile.js, from the SDK version and the kind of LG's Home app as it came with the TV. Share log and GET /api/tv show what was decided.

webOS 5 and 6 (SDK 5, 6) webOS 22 and 23 (SDK 7, 8) webOS 24 to 26 (SDK 9 to 11)
LG's Home app (reports) native program native program flutter or web app; native on a 43UR75006LK (SDK 9.2.2)
How the launcher becomes Home stand-in over LG's Home program; the app manager is not restarted stand-in, as on webOS 5 and 6 launcher folder mounted over LG's Home app and the app manager restarted; the stand-in where LG's Home is native
Setup on unlisted firmware Set up anyway needed Set up anyway needed continues by itself
Input key Inputs list started on each press started on each press kept ready over the running app on webOS 25 (SDK 10); started on each press on SDK 9 and 11
Picture notifications window overlay overlay topui from SDK 10, overlay on SDK 9
Node.js, browser engine Node.js 8; Chromium 68 (webOS 5) or 79 (webOS 6) Node.js 12 (webOS 23 report) Node.js 16 (webOS 25)

The Home row follows LG's Home app, not the version number: a TV with a native Home gets the stand-in whatever its version, and a flutter or web Home gets the mount. How both work is in What it changes on the TV.
