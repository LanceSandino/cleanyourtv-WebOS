# Dawn: where the picture comes from

`dawn.jpg` (the wallpaper) and `dawn-front.webp` (the same picture with the sky cut away, drawn over the clock) are the "Rainforest
dawn" scene and its cut-out from [Seagey/couch-tv](https://github.com/Seagey/couch-tv) (`tv-home/web/backgrounds/scene-dawn.jpg` and
`scene-dawn-fg.webp`), under that project's MIT licence:

couch-tv's own credits say these scenes were made with an AI image model from text only (no source photo, not a real place) and may
be used however you like. The layout of this theme is CleanYourTV's own, after couch-tv's Dawn look.
